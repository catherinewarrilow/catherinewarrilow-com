About me

Hi, I’m Catherine. I’m in peak midlife and trying to love everything about it. I’m happy when I’m hunting out treasure in a charity shop, reinventing a space at home, creating new outfits for the week ahead, and actually I’m really happy when I’m working too, as I get to go on lots of days out and help attractions and experiences sort out their strategy. Winning.

My downfalls are red wine and ORD (obsessive reinvention disorder). You’ll have to request a chapter from my upcoming book, Don’t Call Me Caroline…

Oh, and in amongst the chaos I’ve become something of an AI expert, and I show people how to build tools to simplify family life.

Soon, in 2027, as well as publishing my book, I’m launching Life Edit: retreats for women who are also in their absolute peak and want to enjoy every minute by figuring out properly what that looks like, with no distractions, self-created or otherwise.

P.S. Did I mention that I also host vision board workshops? Only in Oxfordshire at the moment.

So yes, chaos. But all kinds of incredible, energising, special, kind chaos. The type that lifts people up, gives them a hall pass to be more them and, you know, brings a little extra to the everyday.

Say hi, read a bit, send me a question. I love to hear from you.

Catherine (not Caroline) x
